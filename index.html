Biography

I am a Ph.D. student at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology under the supervision of Prof. Shing-Chi Cheung. I was an M.Phil. student at The University of Hong Kong under the supervision of Dr. Heming Cui and Prof. Cho-Li Wang. My research lies at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Software Engineering.
